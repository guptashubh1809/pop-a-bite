FROM DOGRA KITCHENS

Sund. Jammu's own winter sweet.

A Dogra family recipe of almonds, cashews, dates, coconut and gond, roasted slowly in desi ghee. In Jammu, it turns up at life's biggest moments.

  • Before a baby arrives

    In Dogra families, a mother-to-be's own family sends her Sund in the last month before her baby is born.

  • At Lohri

    When the cold sets in and families gather around the Lohri fire, Sund is part of the celebration.

  • At weddings

    From one family to another, Sund has its place at Dogra weddings too.

Sund has always been a part of our home in Jammu. Maa still makes it the traditional way, with the goodness of jaggery, and now brings the same homemade warmth to yours. Add Sund to your order →

WHAT GOES IN

The same things Maa always used.

Most packaged sweets swap the good stuff for something cheaper. We swap it back.

  • Refined sugar or corn syrup →

    Jaggery

    Sugarcane juice, simply boiled down. It gives ladoos the deep, caramel taste they have always had in Indian homes.

  • Palm oil, vanaspati or seed oils →

    Desi cow ghee

    Flour and dry fruits are roasted slowly in ghee, and ghee is what holds every ladoo together.

  • Maida →

    Whole wheat and chana sattu

    Sattu is roasted chana flour, an everyday staple in many Indian kitchens. It's why our Sattu Ladoo has 11.6 g protein per 100 g.

  • Artificial colour and flavour →

    Real nuts and whole spices

    Almonds, cashews, makhana and coconut you can see in every bite, with cardamom and black pepper for flavour.
