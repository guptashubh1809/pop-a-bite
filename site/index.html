Why we started.

In most Indian homes, ladoos were never bought. Maa made them. She roasted the flour in ghee, melted the jaggery and rolled each one by hand, with a jar kept aside for the kids and another for the grandparents.

Life got busier. Fewer homes have the hours it takes, and what's on the shelf is often made with palm oil, refined sugar and flavourings.

So our Maa makes them for you, in our home kitchen in Jammu, the way she always has. She buys organic ingredients in small batches, never in bulk, starts cooking when your order comes in, and rolls every ladoo by hand.

And that's where Pop-A-Bite began. We believe Indian kitchens have so much more to offer. There are regional sweets that deserve to be discovered, traditional recipes that are rarely found in shops, and familiar flavours waiting for a little experimentation.

We want to explore them all, bringing you something different from time to time, without ever losing the taste, care and warmth of Maa's kitchen.

Here's to a poppin' bite!
