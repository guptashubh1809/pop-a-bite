Our Story.

At Pop-A-Bite, we believe that the joy of eating can remain guilt-free. Our aim is simple: to bring you the comfort of homemade food and the pleasure of sweet bites, without compromising on your health and fitness.

We believe sweetness can be both delicious and wholesome. With quality ingredients, millets in selected recipes and no artificial sweeteners, we create homemade treats with your health in mind. We want you to enjoy the sweetness you love while feeling good about what goes into every bite.

As Pop-A-Bite grows, our commitment remains the same: to keep homemade care, thoughtfully chosen ingredients and healthier sweetness at the heart of everything we make.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before your first bite.

What is Sund?

Sund is a traditional sweet speciality from Jammu, known for its rich blend of dry fruits. Traditionally prepared by roasting nuts and dried fruits in ghee, it is especially popular during the winter months.

How do I order?

Ordering is simple! Message or call us on WhatsApp. Let us know which treats you’d like, the quantity and your delivery location. We’ll confirm availability, delivery details and the total price before you confirm your order.

Where do we deliver?

We currently deliver only in the Jammu region. Share your location or PIN code with us on WhatsApp so we can confirm delivery availability. Delivery charges are additional to the listed product prices and depend on your location. We’ll let you know the exact delivery charge and total cost before you confirm your order.

Where can I find ingredients and allergen details?

Each product has its own ingredient list, allergen information and storage guidance. Please click “Explore this bite” under the product to check these details.
