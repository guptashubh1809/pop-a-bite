Our Story.

At Pop-A-Bite, we believe that the joy of eating can remain guilt-free. Our aim is simple: to bring you the comfort of homemade food and the pleasure of sweet bites, made with ingredients you can read on the label.

We believe sweetness can be both delicious and honest. We make our treats with nuts, cow ghee and jaggery, with whole wheat and chana in selected recipes. There is no palm oil, no refined oil, no refined sugar and no artificial colours, flavours or preservatives, so you can see exactly what goes into every bite.

As Pop-A-Bite grows, our commitment remains the same: to keep homemade care, thoughtfully chosen ingredients and honest sweetness at the heart of everything we make.

We are excited to see where Pop-A-Bite takes us next!

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before your first bite.

What is Sund?

Sund is a sweet speciality from Jammu, made with a rich blend of dry fruits roasted in ghee and popular in the winter months.

How do I order?

Ordering is simple! Use the order builder above, or message or call us on WhatsApp. Let us know which treats you’d like, the quantity and your delivery location. We’ll confirm availability, delivery details and the total price before you confirm your order. Payment is by UPI QR code.

Where do we deliver?

We currently deliver only in the Jammu region. Share your location or PIN code with us on WhatsApp so we can confirm delivery availability. Delivery charges are additional to the listed product prices and depend on your location. We’ll let you know the exact delivery charge and total cost before you confirm your order.

Where can I find ingredients and allergen details?

Each product has its own ingredient list, allergen information, storage guidance and, for the ladoos, a nutrition table per 100 g. Please click “Explore this bite” under the product to check these details.
