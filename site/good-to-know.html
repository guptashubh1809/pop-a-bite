GOOD TO KNOW

A few things before your first bite.

Why does it take up to a day for my order to process?

Because we start only when you order. We get whatever ingredients the order needs in small quantities, then roasts and rolls everything by hand. How long it takes depends on how much you order and how many orders are ahead of yours. We'll tell you the exact date before you pay.

What is Sund?

Sund is a Dogra speciality from Jammu: almonds, cashews, dates, coconut and gond roasted slowly in desi ghee. Families make it in winter and for Lohri, serve it at weddings, and send it to a daughter in the last month before her baby is born. Eat a spoonful as it is, or with a glass of warm milk.

What is Sattu?

Sattu is roasted chana (Bengal gram) ground into flour. It has been an everyday food in Bihar for generations, stirred into drinks, stuffed into parathas and rolled into ladoos, because it's filling and needs no more cooking. Our Sattu Ladoo has 11.6 g protein per 100 g, tested in a lab.

How do I order?

Ordering is simple! Use "Build your order" button above, or message or call us. Let us know which treats you’d like, the quantity and your delivery location. We’ll confirm availability, delivery details and the total price before you confirm your order. Payment is by UPI QR code.

Where do we deliver?

We currently deliver only in the Jammu region. Share your location or PIN code with us so we can confirm delivery availability. Delivery charges are additional to the listed product prices and depend on your location. We’ll let you know the exact delivery charge and total cost before you confirm your order.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes, pickup from our home kitchen in Jammu is free. Choose "Pickup" in the order builder, and we'll send the exact location and a pickup time on once your order is ready.

Can I order for my family in Jammu?

Yes. Wherever you live, you can order for parents, grandparents or friends in Jammu. Share their address with us and we'll deliver to them.

How long do they keep?

Up to 3 months. Store them at room temperature, away from direct sunlight.

Where can I find ingredients and allergen details?

Each product has its own ingredient list, allergen information, storage guidance and, for the ladoos, a nutrition table per 100 g. Please click “Explore this bite” under the product to check these details.
